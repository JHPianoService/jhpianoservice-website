01

Tuning & Performance

Improve pitch, tone, touch, and consistency across the instrument.

  • Piano tuning

    Routine, seasonal, and corrective tuning.

  • Voicing

    Shape and balance the piano’s tone.

  • Action regulation

    Refine touch, response, and evenness.

02

Repairs & Maintenance

Address everyday problems, worn components, and unusual mechanical needs.

  • General repairs

    Keys, pedals, action noise, and other mechanical concerns.

  • Keytop repair & replacement

    Repair or replace damaged and worn key coverings.

  • Hammer replacement

    Replace worn hammers when repair is no longer practical.

  • Restringing

    Replace broken, corroded, or aging strings.

  • Piano cleaning

    Careful interior and instrument-appropriate cleaning.

  • Custom obsolete-part fabrication

    Create solutions when original parts are unavailable.

  • Pest-damage assessment

    Evaluate and repair instrument damage; extermination may require a licensed specialist.

03

Restoration

Renew treasured instruments with respect for their age, character, and intended use.

  • Rebuilding

    Comprehensive renewal of major internal systems.

  • Refinishing

    Restore and protect the instrument’s exterior.

  • Complete restoration

    Coordinate structural, mechanical, and cosmetic work.

  • Historical instrument restoration

    Careful work guided by the instrument’s period and construction.

  • Antique instrument care

    Assessment and service for heirloom and antique instruments.

04

Specialty Services

Specialized repair and straightforward guidance beyond standard piano care.

  • Piano buying & selling guidance

    Practical evaluations and advice before a decision.
