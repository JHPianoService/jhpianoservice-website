The approach

Care that respects the piano—and the people who play it.

My journey as a piano technician began with my father, Noah Hébert, a skilled piano craftsman who introduced me to the trade through an apprenticeship that became the foundation of my career. Beyond teaching me the technical side of piano care, my father passed down the values of patience, precision, craftsmanship, and dedication that continue to guide my work today.

For more than four decades, I have dedicated myself to the art of piano tuning, repair, and maintenance. Every piano has its own character, and my goal is to help each instrument perform at its best while preserving the qualities that make it unique.

Throughout my career, I have had the privilege of serving a wide range of clients, including music stores, colleges, casinos, symphony orchestras, piano teachers, musicians, churches, and private piano owners.

Offices based in Biloxi, MS and New Iberia, LA, with service throughout Southern Louisiana and Southern Mississippi, I am committed to providing dependable, professional piano care built on generations of craftsmanship and experience.
